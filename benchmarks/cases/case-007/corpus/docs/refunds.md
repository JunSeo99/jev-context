# Subscription refund decisions
Effective: 2026-05-01

Used subscription periods are normally not refundable.
A settled duplicate of the same renewal is refunded in full.
The duplicate exception applies even when the valid period was used.
Refund the receipt marked repeats, leaving the original renewal paid.
The amount is the duplicate receipt's amount_minor in its currency.

## Access and credits
A duplicate correction does not cancel seats or change renewal dates.
Remove only credits actually granted by the duplicate transaction.
A duplicate with zero extra credits requires no credit adjustment.
[Money movement](money.md) controls approval and timing language.

## Other cases
A voluntary downgrade takes effect at the next renewal.
A tax-invoice correction does not itself create a cash refund.
Chargebacks are handled by a separate dispute workflow.
These rules do not authorize an agent to send money automatically.
