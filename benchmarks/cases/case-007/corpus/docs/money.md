# Money movement communications
Current operational standard

A support draft must distinguish eligibility from executed payment.
Finance review is required before a refund is submitted to the processor.
After submission, advise an estimated 5–10 business days for bank posting.
Never guarantee a bank-arrival date; issuers control posting time.
Do not say a refund has been processed without a processor refund id.

## Privacy
Quote receipt numbers, currency, and amount in the support reply.
Do not include processor event ids or card fingerprints in customer text.
The public case id is suitable for follow-up.

## Routing
Escalations enter the finance-review queue.
A named reviewer is assigned after queue intake, not at draft time.
Internal queue membership is not included in public replies.
Payment credentials are never requested by support email.
