# 2024 billing support notes
Archived; replaced by May 2026 policy

Early guidance offered account credit for duplicate card charges.
Agents sometimes promised three business days for every refund.
Neither practice is the current operating rule.
Seat cancellations previously required a separate support ticket.

## Product changes discussed
The receipt page gained a printable view.
The billing owner can now change the invoice address.
Currency selection still occurs at workspace creation.
A per-seat refund calculator was proposed for future review.
That proposal did not change duplicate-charge policy.
