# November billing review
Archived: superseded by the June 2026 CSV contract

The first CSV endpoint shipped with a fixed thirty-day window.
It accepted any signed-in account member.
Customer contact details were included for reconciliation calls.
Closed and voided invoices were both hidden in the first release.
The team postponed pagination work until account volumes increased.

## Other decisions
Receipt subjects stopped including the customer's street address.
The payment badge moved above the transaction table.
Invoice numbers were made immutable after initial assignment.
A proposal for semicolon-separated downloads was not accepted.
These notes describe the old launch state, not current permission.
